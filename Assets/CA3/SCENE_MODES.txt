# Quick scene modes (Inspector)

## After importing your Menagerie arm (Kinova Gen3)
- ArmAgent: names default to joint_1..joint_7 / bracelet_link (check Console for "not found")
- ArmJointController: homeTargets + jointRanges already set for Gen3 (match train_controller.py)

## Collect rollout
- ArmJointController: emitActions=true (random joint goals)
- Disable: WorldModelController, IkGoalProvider
- Enable: RolloutRecorder
- Optional: disable WorldModelDriver

## Check controller on random goals (Step 3)
- Enable WorldModelController (controller.json); IkGoalProvider disabled
- Disable RolloutRecorder

## Live tracking (after training)
- Create Sphere "TrackingTarget" (remove its collider) -> wire into IkGoalProvider.target
- Enable IkGoalProvider + WorldModelController (controller.json)
- ArmJointController: emitActions=false (WorldModelController does this automatically; IK turns off resampleGoals)
- Disable RolloutRecorder
